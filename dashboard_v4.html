The xB Index is a composite process score built from 10 metrics, percentile-ranked against the 24-club Championship population. Tier 1 (shot creation and suppression) carries 50% of the weight; Tier 2 (build-up and progression) 30%; Tier 3 (set-piece and pressing context) 20%.

xG per Shot ATK and xG per Shot DEF are shown as raw reference values, not scored — they're the outcome the process metrics drive toward. The stated target is 0.12 xG per shot created, the level Hull and Brentford hit in their respective promotion seasons. Click any column header to sort.
