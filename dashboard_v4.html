Methodology — what's scored, what isn't

What's new in this version. Three changes from the previous dashboard: (1) H1 Away rank is no longer scored — it's a results-derived metric (W/D/L/Pts), which contradicts the process-only positioning. It may return as context, but never as an index input. (2) Cross accuracy % is now scored in T2 — cross volume stays displayed-but-not-scored (it's a style choice), but cross accuracy measures execution and is a fair process signal. (3) Shots in box % added to T1 — a shot-selection signal that complements xG per shot.

xG per shot is now non-penalty (this version). Both the attacking and defending halves of the xG-per-shot differential now exclude penalties on both sides. A penalty is a near-guaranteed high-value chance handed out by a decision, not created or conceded through open-play process — leaving them in rewards or punishes sides for winning or conceding spot-kicks rather than for shot-creation or -suppression quality. Note: the Birmingham target for attacking xG per shot (currently 0.12) was calibrated against the old with-penalty scale and now needs re-checking against real non-penalty peer-band data before it can be trusted — treat it as stale until then.

The xB Index ranks each club against the other 23 on 13 process metrics across three weighted tiers (T1 shot quality 50% · T2 build-up 30% · T3 context 20%). Each metric produces a score from 0 to 100 — 100 = best in league, 50 = middle of the pack, 0 = worst. For metrics where lower is better (like PPDA and shots conceded), the direction is flipped before scoring, so a higher score always means better process. When you tap a team, you'll see their rank out of 24 for every metric, which is easier to grasp than the raw percentile. Outcomes are excluded by design: no goals, no points, no win/draw/loss records anywhere in the inputs. Cross volume is displayed but not scored — how much a team crosses tells you about style, not quality. Wage-bill ranks are estimates compiled from published accounts (source & date shown here in production). Where a data point is missing for a club it shows as “—” and is excluded from that club's score, never silently imputed.
