Methodology — what's scored, what isn't

The xB Index ranks each club against the other 23 on 11 process metrics across three weighted tiers (T1 shot quality 50% · T2 build-up 30% · T3 context 20%). Each metric produces a score from 0 to 100 — 100 = best in league, 50 = middle of the pack, 0 = worst. For metrics where lower is better (like PPDA and shots conceded), the direction is flipped before scoring, so a higher score always means better process. When you tap a team, you'll see their rank out of 24 for every metric, which is easier to grasp than the raw percentile. Outcomes are excluded by design: no goals, no points, no win/draw/loss records anywhere in the inputs. Cross volume is displayed but not scored — how much a team crosses tells you about style, not quality. Wage-bill ranks are estimates compiled from published accounts (source & date shown here in production). Where a data point is missing for a club it shows as “—” and is excluded from that club's score, never silently imputed.
