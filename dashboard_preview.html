The xB Index is a composite process score built from 12 metrics, percentile-ranked against the 24-club Championship population. Tier 1 (shot creation and suppression) carries 50% of the weight; Tier 2 (build-up and progression) 30%; Tier 3 (set-piece and pressing context) 20%.

xG per Shot ATK and xG per Shot DEF are shown as reference values, not scored. The article's stated target is 0.12 xG per shot created, the level Hull and Brentford hit in their respective promotion seasons. Click any column header to sort.

v3: Tier band moved inside the table — guaranteed alignment. All columns now sortable: click a header to sort, click again to reverse. Default sort is xB Index descending.
